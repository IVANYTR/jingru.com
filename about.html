About the company公司简介

A Ningbo textile company that both makes and exports. 一家在宁波既生产、也做出口的纺织公司。

Ningbo Jinrun Import & Export Co., Ltd. is based in Ningbo. Benefiting from the mature textile industrial cluster of the Yangtze River Delta and superior port foreign trade advantages, we are an integrated manufacturing and trading enterprise specializing in textiles. We independently produce a wide range of garment accessories and fabrics including shell fabrics, lining fabrics, interlinings, zippers, reflective tapes, visors and more. We provide global apparel clients with services covering sampling & customization, production supply, one-stop sourcing and export.

宁波槿润进出口有限公司位于宁波。依托长三角成熟的纺织产业集群和便利的港口外贸条件，我们是一家专注纺织品的工贸一体企业。公司自主生产面料、里布、衬布、拉链、反光带、帽檐等多种服装面辅料，为全球服装客户提供打样与定制、生产供应、一站式采购和出口服务。

Why Ningbo为什么在宁波

The Yangtze River Delta has a mature textile industry, so sampling and follow-up supply sit close to material sources. Ningbo’s port is the export door for finished accessories and fabrics.

长三角纺织产业成熟，打样和后续供货都靠近原料与加工配套。宁波港口则是面辅料出口的出海口。
